🏛️ Central Government: latest updates

India now runs on four Labour Codes. Key dates so far.

DateUpdate
21 Nov 2025All four Labour Codes brought into force (Wages, Industrial Relations, Social Security, OSH).
8 May 2026Final Rules under the four Codes notified.
29 May 2026EPF wage ceiling fixed at ₹15,000 under the Social Security Code. Interest on delayed dues set at 12% a year.
1 Jul 2026Fresh EPF, EPS and EDLI Schemes, 2026 notified.
17 Sep 2026EPF wage ceiling raised from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 per month (S.O. 5109(E)). For September 2026, contribution is split: 1 to 16 Sep on ₹15,000 and 17 to 30 Sep on ₹25,000.
UnchangedESIC wage ceiling stays at ₹21,000 per month.
Existing PF members earning above the ceiling are not affected. Always check the latest EPFO and Ministry notices before filing.

⚖️ The four Labour Codes

29 old labour laws are now merged into four Codes, in force since 21 November 2025.

💰 Code on Wages

One definition of wages, bonus and equal pay. National floor wage idea.

🤝 Industrial Relations Code

Trade unions, strikes, layoffs, standing orders and fixed-term employment.

🛡️ Social Security Code

PF, ESIC, gratuity and maternity in one law. Includes gig and platform workers.

👷 OSH Code

Safety, health and working conditions, plus licences.

Final Rules were notified in May 2026, but states are still publishing their own rules and thresholds. Confirm your state's position on its labour portal.
