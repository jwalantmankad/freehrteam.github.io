💰 1. Salary structure: the 50% rule

Under the Code on Wages, Basic + DA should be at least 50% of total pay. PF, ESIC and gratuity are all worked out on it.

Simple rule

  • Basic = 50% of Gross, or the state minimum Basic if that is higher.
  • The rest can be HRA and other allowances.
  • Gujarat minimum Basic used on this site: ₹13,600.

Example: Gross ₹30,000

Basic + DA (50%)₹15,000
Allowances₹15,000
Employee PF 12%₹1,800
Employee ESICNot applicable
Minimum wages are set by each state by zone and skill, and the variable DA is revised twice a year (usually April and October). Daily rate = monthly rate ÷ 26.

🧾 4. Professional Tax and Labour Welfare Fund

Professional Tax

A state tax on salary. Delhi, Haryana, Rajasthan, UP, Uttarakhand and Himachal do not charge it. The national yearly cap is ₹2,500.

Gujarat: nil up to ₹12,000 gross, ₹200 a month above that.

Labour Welfare Fund

A small yearly or half-yearly contribution from employee and employer. It applies in many states, including Gujarat, and the amount and date differ by state. See the State section on the Home page.

🎁 5. Gratuity, bonus and wages

Gratuity

10+ employees. Paid after 5 years (1 year for fixed-term staff). Formula: last Basic + DA × 15 ÷ 26 × years. The maximum is ₹20 lakh.

Example: ₹20,000 × 15 ÷ 26 × 6 years = about ₹69,231.

Bonus

20+ employees. At least 8.33% of wages, up to 20% in a profitable year, paid within 8 months of the financial year end.

Wage payment

Monthly wages should be paid by the 7th of the next month. Give a payslip. Overtime is paid at twice the normal rate.

Other laws

POSH committee (10+ staff), maternity leave of 26 weeks for the first two children, and a creche at 50+ staff.

✅ 7. New joiner checklist

Documents to collect

  • Aadhaar, PAN and bank details
  • Existing UAN, if any
  • Passport photo and address proof
  • Education and previous employer papers

What to give and register

  • Appointment letter with the salary breakup
  • UAN and PF Form 11
  • ESIC insurance number, if eligible
  • Attendance, wage register and payslip every month

❓ Frequently asked questions

Is PF compulsory for every employee?

Once your establishment has 20 or more employees, PF applies to staff earning up to the wage ceiling. New joiners above the ceiling can be left out.

Can I pay less than the state minimum Basic?

No. If 50% of Gross is lower than the minimum, raise Basic to the minimum. The calculator does this for you.

Is ESIC counted on Gross or Basic?

This site's calculator uses Basic + DA, and only when it is below ₹21,000. Check your ESIC office if you are unsure.

Which dates matter most?

The 7th for salary and TDS, and the 15th for PF and ESIC.

Where do I see the newest circulars?

On the Home page under Government circulars, or on the EPFO, ESIC and Ministry of Labour websites.

Rates, ceilings and dates change often. Confirm on the official portal before you pay or file.
